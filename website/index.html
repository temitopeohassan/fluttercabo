The app

Book in under a minute

  1. Tell us where you're going. Pick a hotel, a landmark or one of our tours.
  2. See the price up front. In naira, with an estimate in your home currency.
  3. Meet a vetted driver. Check the plate, share your trip, and pay by card, wallet or cash.

Safety first

Built for people new to the city

Lagos is one of Africa's great cities. We make sure your first visit feels as safe as your tenth.

  • Vetted drivers ID, police and driving record checks, plus vehicle inspections.
  • Trip PIN Your ride only starts when the driver enters your 4-digit PIN.
  • Live trip sharing Send a link anyone can open, no app needed.
  • 24/7 SOS One tap alerts emergency services, your contacts and our support team.
